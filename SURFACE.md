# Flow2Short Studio — Workspace Surface

## Scope and mode

Operate: a single offline-first editing workspace for assembling daily Google Flow clips into a finished vertical short.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The whole recipe stays visible. The surface refuses the generic multi-track professional-editor metaphor and turns each clip into a clear, reversible production decision.

**OWN-WORLD:** Warm white work surface, ink-navy typography, emerald actions and coral audio accents. Vazirmatn carries the RTL interface. Quiet 14px-radius panels, precise one-pixel rules, restrained depth and consistent outline icons make it feel like a purpose-built desktop tool.

**STORY:** Import clips, arrange and trim them, choose exactly how much original Flow audio survives, add narration/music/captions, then render locally. Privacy and recoverability stay visible throughout.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Compact header across the top, four-step rail on the right, import and clip decisions in the central work surface, and a persistent export summary at the bottom. Desktop reveals the whole recipe; mobile collapses it into one readable column with the final action always reachable.

**FORM:** Approved image-led product workspace; the user explicitly approved the generated desktop-and-mobile comp on 2026-09-22. Signature interaction: imported clips settle into a reorderable stack while per-clip source-audio levels remain visibly attached to each clip.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Constraints

- Persian RTL interface; English filenames remain LTR.
- Desktop rendering is the reliable path; iPhone is best-effort and must preserve the project on memory failure.
- User media never leaves the device.
- The approved comp at `design/flow2short-approved-comp.png` is the visual authority.
